Kafka Banking Notification Aggregation

JWT batching & stream processing for a banking client

Problem

Banking client needed aggregation of messages arriving through a Kafka queue. Each message required a JWT, but the client imposed rate limits on JWT usage. Processing every message independently introduced unnecessary latency and limited throughput.

Constraints

  • JWT rate limits from the client
  • Message ordering potentially important
  • Failure handling needed to be reliable

Approach

Aggregation layer allowing multiple messages to share a single JWT, enabling batch processing and reducing token-related overhead.

Implementation

Secondary programmer on the implementation. The bank designed the solution; Mark's team implemented it and provided documentation intended to help troubleshoot/debug the system.

Result

Implementation worked on the team's machines and was deployed for the client. No specific production performance feedback from the client — no metrics invented or implied.

Lessons

Batching behind a shared auth token is a valid pattern when rate limits are the bottleneck. The aggregation approach separates concerns: one token per batch, not one per message.

Banking Microservices — Baseline Templates

Customer data, points transactions, budgeting & subscription flows

Problem

Client needed baseline/template services as starting points for client-specific microservices. These are customized to fulfill specific functions within the client's engine and provide banks with the data needed to drive their features.

Approach

Built reusable service templates handling customer account data, points transactions, and UI/process flow for budgeting and subscription management modules.

Implementation

Custom hooks that react when a customer's transaction satisfies a business rule and then modify/update parts of the client's data. For example, a qualifying transaction on a particular date might award points the customer can later redeem through another service.

Result

Baseline services providing banks with the data needed to drive their features. Event-driven Kafka processing including a pipeline that aggregates notification data.

Logistics Business Automation Platform

2015–2018 · Multi-module business system

Problem

A logistics company had complicated, partly manual business processes across HR/payroll, operations, accounting, and purchasing that needed turning into integrated software workflows.

Constraints

  • Multiple business domains in one system
  • Digitizing manual processes
  • Integration across modules

Approach

Built a web-based business system covering all major operational areas with integrated workflows.

Implementation

  • HR and Payroll: Employee onboarding, payroll processing, pay calculations, tax calculations
  • Operations: Contract tracking, status updates, maritime process tracking, trucking process tracking
  • Accounting and Finance: Chart of accounts, bookkeeping, credit/debit transactions, cheque tracking
  • Purchasing: Quote tracking, approval workflows, purchase tracking

Result

Integrated software workflows replacing manual processes. Recurring professional focus: taking complicated, partly manual business processes and turning them into integrated software workflows.
